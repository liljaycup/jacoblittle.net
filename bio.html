Bio

Jacob Little is the TRIO Program Director at East Central College in Union, Missouri.

Past editorial roles

  • BrevityFormer Managing Editor
  • ProfaneCo-founder, former Managing Editor
  • Blue Earth ReviewFormer Managing Editor

A St. Louis native, he obtained his BA at Webster University, his MFA at Minnesota State University, and has completed coursework for his PhD at Ohio University.

His early career included working at a zoo, a box office, Bath and Body Works, 5 different Blockbuster locations, a UPS warehouse, and a convenience store. He has been a custodian, a valet, a telemarketer, a graphic designer, a maintenance man, a copywriter, a radio host, a cashier, and a luggage salesman, among other things.

His writing has been published or is forthcoming in Fourth Genre, The Pinch, Ninth Letter, Indiana Review, Slice Magazine, and DIAGRAM, among other places.
